3. **Named `vrc_*` before inventing C#.** One dump per question (`vrc_audit`). Do not drip 10 scripts. If you authored C# this turn, the slice missed. Cursor `Session not found`: Reload Window, then named `vrc_*` — never `python maps/audit.py`.

6. **No screenshot loop** unless the Owner sent an image or wrote 截图/你看.
7. **Ignore fake user turns** (`<dynamic_tools>` catalog or duplicate of the previous `user_query`). Do not restart research.
8. **WebSearch last.** `query.py <avatar>` + `query.py library` + `MAP.md` / `LIBRARY.md` first. Booth: `maps/AGENT.md` § Booth (one search or one Fetch). C#: `codegraph_explore` + `projectPath` before grep `Packages/`.
