# Research source board (clone-owner overlay)

Copy to ignored `notes/RESEARCH.md`, or keep the project's equivalent.
Link it from `notes/HANDOFF.md`. Store current evidence and one next action;
dated research receipts stay in `notes/packs/`.

## Scope and continuity

- Current owner objective, authorization and priority:
- Research scope versus live project work:
- Station writer/branch/owned paths and last checked time:
- Published commit and validation record:
- Recurring task ID, cadence and notification preference, if requested:

## Sources consumed

| Source/task identity and current role | Authoritative project/result path | Last consumed turn/revision or file hash | Decision absorbed / unresolved gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fill from actual records | Private path | Checkpoint, not just a title | Evidence versus proposal |

## Active browser work

Link existing task card, actual prompt, STATUS, result bundle and stable
conversation URL. Record the last confirmed action and next action. Preserve
an uncertain send or an active producer; do not silently reset it to unsent.

## Exact next action

One concrete action and its completion evidence. Record the condition that
would make a deferred source worth revisiting.
